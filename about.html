Insurance built by people who've filed a claim and hated it.

Stackly started as a frustrated group of engineers, actuaries, and one very persistent claims adjuster who thought coverage shouldn't require a hold-music playlist.

Our story

From a spreadsheet of complaints to a company.

In 2019, our founders kept a running spreadsheet of every insurance complaint they heard from friends: buried exclusions, agents who vanished after the sale, claims that took months to resolve. The list got long enough that it became a business plan.

We built Stackly around one idea: coverage should be as easy to see and adjust as a grocery cart. Add a layer, drop a layer, watch the price change in real time — no phone tree required.

Today we underwrite policies across the continental US alongside six carrier partners, but the spreadsheet mentality never left. Every feature we ship still has to answer one question: does this make a bad day easier?

What we stand for

Four things we won't compromise on.

Total transparency

Every price is itemized by layer. If we can't explain a fee in one sentence, we don't charge it.

Speed that respects you

Quotes in minutes, claims decisions in days — your time is worth more than our process.

Control, always

Add or drop a layer whenever your life changes. No agent gatekeeping the toggle.

Fair when it counts

Claims are reviewed by people, not just algorithms, and every denial comes with a real explanation.

How we got here

Six years, four layers, one mission.

2019

The complaint spreadsheet

Three friends start tracking every bad insurance experience they hear about. It becomes the founding brief.

2021

Auto layer launches

Stackly opens for business with a single layer — auto — in three states, underwritten with Meridian Mutual.

2022

Home and Health added

The stack grows to three layers. Bundle pricing goes live, and the mobile app ships.

2024

Life layer completes the stack

All four layers are available nationwide, alongside six underwriting partners.

2026

240,000 policies and counting

Stackly pays out $18M in claims in a single year while keeping average resolution time under 48 hours.
